# Client Onboarding FAQ

## How does an engagement start?

Every engagement starts with a short scoping call. We agree on a narrow,
buildable slice of the client's backlog - something that matters but is not on
the critical path. That slice becomes a paid two-week pilot.

## What does the client get during the pilot?

Working software at the end of two weeks, plus a written update every working
day. Code samples or a short walkthrough video are available before the pilot
starts, on request.

## What happens after the pilot?

If the pilot speaks for itself, we scope the next build. If not, the client
keeps the code and walks away. There is no retainer and no lock-in.
